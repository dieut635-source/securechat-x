SecureChat is a private messaging and calling app built on Matrix, the open standard for secure, decentralised communication.

SecureChat connects to https://chat.securechat.com.au by default and is designed for both personal use and managed Android deployments.

Stay connected with:
 • End-to-end encrypted messages and rooms
 • Voice and video calls
 • Reactions, replies, polls and pinned messages
 • File, image and voice-message sharing
 • Synchronised message history across your signed-in devices
 • A SecureChat web client at https://chat.securechat.com.au

<b>Privacy first</b>
SecureChat does not include a preconfigured analytics, crash-reporting or remote bug-report upload endpoint. Your organisation can manage access and sharing policies through Android managed configurations.

<b>Built for managed deployments</b>
The homeserver is fixed to SecureChat and in-app account creation is disabled. Administrators can further restrict file sending and configure automatic logout after the app has remained in the background.

<b>Open protocol</b>
SecureChat uses Matrix for interoperable, real-time communication. The Android source is available at https://github.com/dieut635-source/securechat-x.

The app uses USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT so incoming call notifications can be presented while the device is locked.
